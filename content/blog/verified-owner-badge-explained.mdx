You'll see a small green badge — **Verified Owner** — on some listings.
Here's what it means.

## What we verify

- **Aadhaar** — the seller's name matches the document. We don't share Aadhaar
  numbers with buyers, ever.
- **Phone number ownership** — the OTP must come from the same SIM associated
  with the Aadhaar identity.
- **Property document** — at least one of: sale deed, RERA registration, or owner NoC.

## What we don't check (yet)

- Property condition (you should always visit before paying).
- Whether the price is fair (use our [EMI calculator](/loans) and locality insights).
- Whether the seller will respond quickly — that varies.

## Why it matters

Listings with the badge get on average **3.4× more leads** because buyers know
the basics are real. We also auto-deprioritise unverified listings in search
when there's a verified equivalent nearby.

## Getting verified

If you're a seller, the badge is free. Just upload your documents at
[/auth/verify-docs](/auth/verify-docs). Our team reviews within 24 hours.
