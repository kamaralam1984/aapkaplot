Buying your first flat in Kolkata is exciting — and intimidating. Prices vary
3× across micro-markets, paperwork has improved post-RERA but still has gotchas,
and the right neighbourhood depends on where you (or your kids) need to be.

## How much does a typical 2 BHK cost?

In 2026, here is roughly where the action is for ready-to-move 2 BHK flats:

- **Salt Lake (Sector V)** — ₹85 L – 1.4 Cr. Strong rental demand from IT.
- **New Town (Action Area I/II)** — ₹55 L – 1.1 Cr. Newer infra, lots of supply.
- **Sodepur / Madhyamgram** — ₹35 – 55 L. Best value if you don't need a daily city commute.
- **Behala / Tollygunge** — ₹50 – 80 L. Metro Line 3 phase opens up new options.
- **Howrah riverside** — ₹40 – 70 L. Underrated for the price.

## Five paperwork checks before paying token money

1. **Title chain** — last 30 years of ownership history, clean of disputes.
2. **RERA registration** — Look up the project on the [West Bengal RERA portal](https://hira.wb.gov.in).
3. **Mutation & assessment** — Confirms the seller's name on municipal records.
4. **No-objection from housing society** — required for resale flats.
5. **Encumbrance certificate** — proves no liens / pending loans.
